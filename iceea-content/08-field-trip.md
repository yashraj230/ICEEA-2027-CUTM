# Field Trip & Geological Sites

Educational Geological Field Visit – ICEEA 2026
29th to 30th October - 2026

## Gold Mine & Geological Excursion

The conference includes an educational field visit to the **Hutti Gold Mines** and a geological site excursion near the **Central University of Karnataka (CUK)** to provide hands-on learning experiences.

The field trip is designed to bridge theoretical knowledge with real-world geological and environmental applications. It enhances observational skills, field-based analysis, and interdisciplinary understanding.

### Objectives
- Insights into gold mineralization and mining geology
- Environmental management practices in mining regions
- Study of lithological and geomorphological formations
- Structural geology and Anthropocene-driven environmental changes
- Regional geology of Kalaburagi basin

### Registration Fee
- **Indian Participants:** ₹1,500/-
- **International Participants:** USD $35.00

### Additional Information

**Transportation:** Conference-organized transportation will be provided from CUK campus to Hutti Gold Mines and geological sites.

**Safety Measures:** Participants will be provided safety helmets and instructions during mine visits. All field protocols will follow mining safety guidelines.

**Field Documentation:** Participants are encouraged to maintain field notebooks for geological observations, stratigraphic logging, and structural interpretation.
