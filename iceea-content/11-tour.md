## Travel Tips for International Visitors

- **Visa:** Most countries require e-Visa (apply online 4-7 days before arrival)
- **Currency:** Indian Rupee (₹). Credit cards widely accepted in cities
- **Language:** English is widely spoken in tourist areas
- **Best Season:** October to March for most destinations
- **Transportation:** Domestic flights, trains, and app-based cabs available
- **Safety:** India is generally safe; use registered taxis and stay in tourist areas
