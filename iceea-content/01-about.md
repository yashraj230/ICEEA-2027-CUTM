# ABOUT ICEEA - ICETS 2026

International Conference on Earth and Environment in the Anthropocene

## About ICEEA - 2026

ICEEA-2026 aims to bring together researchers, academicians, environmentalists, policymakers, and industry leaders to address complex Earth and environmental challenges emerging during the Anthropocene epoch. The conference promotes interdisciplinary dialogue focusing on climate change, sustainability, natural resources, geosciences, environmental degradation, and advanced technological solutions.

This international conference actively convenes the world's leading researchers, faculty, students, and industry professionals to disseminate, debate, and catalyze innovative solutions for the most pressing challenges in Earth and Environmental Sciences.

## About ICETS - 2026

ICETS is an internationally recognized congress focusing on environmental and territorial studies. The 18th edition continues its mission to build scientific collaborations between nations for sustainable environmental governance and resilient socio-ecological systems.

In collaboration with the 18th International Congress on Environmental & Territorial Studies (ICETS-2026), this conference creates an unprecedented platform for global collaboration on environmental and territorial challenges.

## Primary Objectives

- Share groundbreaking research and transformative ideas
- Establish prolific partnerships across academic, scientific, and industrial sectors
- Bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application
- Create long-term global networks that drive impactful research and sustainable innovation
- Gain exposure to cutting-edge technologies, methodologies, and international best practices

## Conference Goals

- Address global environmental challenges of the Anthropocene
- Strengthen international collaboration in Earth and environmental sciences
- Promote sustainable development and geo-environmental solutions
- Integrate geoscience, environmental policy, and technology
- Exchange cutting-edge research in climate change, geology, biodiversity, energy, and territorial studies

## Conference Vision

The conference program will feature keynote lectures by eminent scientists and presentations of peer-reviewed original research from participants worldwide. ICEEA-2026 aspires to become a landmark forum for shaping future directions in environmental resilience and sustainable development.
