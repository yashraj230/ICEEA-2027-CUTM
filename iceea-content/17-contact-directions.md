## Travel Tips

- Driving time (approx.): Hyderabad → Kalaburagi ~ 4.5–6 hours depending on route & traffic. Bengaluru → Kalaburagi ~ 8–10 hours (or faster by train/flight combinations).
- Nearest major airports: Hyderabad (Rajiv Gandhi Intl.) and Bengaluru (Kempegowda Intl.). Consider overnight travel or morning departures for day-of arrival.
- Fieldwork participants: pre-register for the field visit due to limited slots; transport details will be shared closer to the conference.
