# Venue & Travel

## Conference Venue

**Central University of Karnataka**
Kalaburagi, Karnataka, India

## How to Reach

### By Air
Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) - 550 km from Central University of Karnataka, Kalaburagi, India.

### By Train
Gulbarga City Railway Station to Bus stand - 1-2 km. From buses connect to the Central University of Karnataka. Travel time: 45 minutes.

### Local Transport
- **Buses:** KSRTC buses available
- **Cabs:** Uber, Ola readily available
