# Review Process — ICEEA-2026 & ICETS-2026

Transparent, fair and rigorous peer review to ensure high scientific standards and integrity.

Publication Tracks: Springer Book & Scopus Journal

## Overview

All full papers submitted to ICEEA-2026/ICETS-2026 will be evaluated through a multi-stage peer-review workflow. The process is designed to be objective, constructive and timely. Final publication decisions are based exclusively on reviewer recommendations.

### Review Stages

1. **Initial Screening** — Administrative and scope check for originality, completeness, and adherence to submission guidelines.
2. **Assignment to Reviewers** — Each manuscript is assigned to at least two independent experts with relevant subject-matter expertise.
3. **Peer Review** — Reviewers assess the manuscript using a standard rubric (novelty, methodology, clarity, significance, reproducibility, references).
4. **Editorial Decision** — Based on reviewer reports, the Editorial Committee issues a decision: Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, or Reject.
5. **Revision & Re-evaluation** — Authors submit revised manuscripts addressing reviewer comments; revised versions may be re-reviewed.
6. **Final Acceptance & Publication Assignment** — Accepted papers are routed to either the Springer edited book series or the Scopus-indexed journal track based on quality, scope, and strategic fit.

### Timeline (Indicative)

- Acceptance notification target: 1 December 2025
- Full paper submission deadline: 30 June 2026

### Reviewer Assignment & Confidentiality

Reviewers are selected for expertise and lack of conflicts of interest. The review process will be **single-anonymous** by default (reviewers know authors, authors do not know reviewers). Reviewer identities are confidential; reviewer comments are shared with authors after anonymisation.

### Decision Categories

Accept | Minor Revision | Major Revision | Reject

### Appeals Policy

If authors believe a decision was reached unfairly, they may submit a concise appeal within **14 days** of the decision. Appeals must summarise reasons and new evidence; appeals are reviewed by an independent committee whose decision is final.

### Review Criteria & Scoring Rubric

Reviewers score manuscripts on a 1–5 scale (1 = Poor, 5 = Excellent):

- **Novelty** — Scientific originality and contribution to the field.
- **Methodology** — Rigor, appropriateness, and reproducibility of methods.
- **Results & Analysis** — Clarity and robustness of results and statistical/analytical treatment.
- **Clarity & Structure** — Organisation, readability, and presentation quality.
- **Significance** — Relevance to conference themes and practical/academic impact.
- **References & Ethics** — Adequacy of literature review, citations, and ethical compliance.

### Reviewer Responsibilities

- Provide an objective, timely and constructive review.
- Declare any conflicts of interest and decline review if conflicted.
- Keep manuscript content confidential and not use it for personal advantage.
- Follow ethical guidelines and report suspected plagiarism or misconduct to the editors.

### Editor & Committee Roles

The Editorial Committee coordinates reviewer selection, resolves disagreements, oversees appeals and ensures final publication decisions follow ethical standards and journal/book requirements.

### Quick Facts

- Minimum reviewers per paper: 2
- Recommended review turnaround: 3–4 weeks
- Default review model: Single-anonymous
- Appeal window: 14 days

### How Papers are Assigned

1. Automated matching by keywords and subject area.
2. Manual assignment by section editors to ensure expertise alignment.
3. Conflict-of-interest checks before final reviewer invitation.
