# Visa Information & Guidance

The **Official Acceptance Letter** issued for the conference may be used to apply for your **Conference Visa**. We strongly recommend applying for a Conference Visa rather than an e-Visa, as e-Visas have previously caused unexpected entry issues into India.

## Quick Actions
- Download Official Acceptance Letter
- Request Acceptance Letter via email
- Contact Organizers

## Recommended Timeline

- **At least 90 days before travel** — Begin visa research and gather documents.
- **60–45 days before travel** — Submit visa application with acceptance letter.
- **30 days before travel** — Follow up with the consulate if required; prepare travel plans.
- **7–14 days before travel** — Confirm visa approval and check entry requirements.

## Recommended Visa Category

Apply for a **Conference Visa** for attending ICEEA-2026 / ICETS-2026. This is the appropriate category for presenters, invited speakers, and delegates. Using an e-Visa is **not** recommended due to past entry issues.

### Typical supporting documents
- Official Acceptance Letter from the conference organizers
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity from date of arrival)
- Completed visa application form for the relevant consulate/embassy
- Recent passport-sized photograph(s) (as per embassy specs)
- Proof of travel (flight bookings) and accommodation (hotel booking or invitation)
- Proof of funds / sponsorship letter (if required by consulate)
- Conference registration receipt / payment proof

### Important Notes
- **Start early:** Visa processing times vary by country—some consulates take several weeks.
- **Check entry rules:** Verify health, vaccination, or testing requirements with the destination consulate and airline prior to travel.
- **Consulate appointment:** Some missions require an in-person appointment—book appointments early.
- **Data accuracy:** Ensure names and passport numbers match the acceptance letter exactly to avoid delays.

## Visa FAQ

**Q: Can I use an e-Visa to attend?**
A: We strongly advise against using e-Visas for this conference. In past years, delegates with e-Visas experienced issues at the port of entry. Please apply for the official Conference Visa instead.

**Q: What if my visa is delayed?**
A: Contact the conference organizers immediately. Provide proof of your application and any consulate communication—we will issue support letters where appropriate.

**Q: Do I need travel insurance?**
A: While not always mandatory, travel and medical insurance is strongly recommended. Some consulates may request proof of insurance—check the consulate guidance for your country.
