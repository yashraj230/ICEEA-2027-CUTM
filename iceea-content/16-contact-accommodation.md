# Guesthouse Accommodation — Central University of Karnataka

Accommodation support is provided for invited speakers, keynote / international speakers, session speakers, and accepted student authors (subject to registration). For queries: iceea@cuk.ac.in

## Eligibility & Policy (Summary)

- **Guaranteed accommodation:** Keynote, invited, international speakers, and registered session speakers.
- **Student authors:** Accommodation is provided to students whose abstracts/full papers were *reviewed and accepted* and who have completed registration.
- **Duration:** Full-conference accommodation (26–30 Oct 2026) may be offered; partial stays are considered case-by-case.
- **Booking process:** Eligible participants must submit a booking request via the form. Final confirmation subject to guesthouse availability.
- **Charges:** Guesthouse is university-managed — accommodation for invited/keynote speakers is complimentary; students may be subsidised (policy to be confirmed).

## Guesthouse Info (CUK)

Central University of Karnataka guesthouse provides basic comfortable lodging for conference participants. Facilities typically include single/double rooms, Wi-Fi, and shared dining. Exact amenities depend on allocation.

For special accommodation requests for VIPs or international delegations, email: iceea@cuk.ac.in

## How allocation works

1. Organizing committee collects booking requests.
2. Eligibility verified against submissions/registration records.
3. Guesthouse rooms assigned and confirmations issued (by email).
4. Late/overflow requests may be accommodated at nearby hotels at participant cost.

## Contact & Support

For booking queries: iceea@cuk.ac.in
