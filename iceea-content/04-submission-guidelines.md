& 18th International Congress on Environmental & Territorial Studies (ICETS) — 26th–30th October, 2026

All submissions undergo rigorous peer review.

## Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished work addressing topics related to the Anthropocene, climate change, sustainability, natural resources, geosciences, territorial studies, environmental degradation and technological solutions. All submissions must be original and not under consideration elsewhere.

### Manuscript Preparation

- Full papers should be prepared in English and follow standard academic formatting (introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusions, references).
- Include an abstract (max 300 words), 3–6 keywords and author affiliations with contact email.
- Word limits: Abstract: max 300 words; Full paper: 4,000–8,000 words (including references) unless specified otherwise.
- Figures and tables should be high resolution and embedded in the manuscript. Provide separate figure files if requested by organisers.

### Peer-review & Publication Opportunities

All submitted full papers will undergo a rigorous peer-review process. Accepted papers will be considered for two publication tracks:

1. **Springer Edited Book (with ISBN)** — Selected, high-quality papers will be published as chapters in a Springer book series to ensure global academic visibility.
2. **Scopus-indexed Journal** — Another set of selected papers will be forwarded for publication in a Scopus-indexed journal (journal details to be announced).

### Ethics & Originality

Authors must ensure submissions are original, not published previously, and not under consideration by any other publication outlet. Plagiarism or duplicate submissions will result in automatic rejection. The final decision on acceptance and publication rests with the peer-review committee.

### Quick Submission Checklist

- Original manuscript, not under review elsewhere.
- Abstract (≤300 words) + keywords.
- Author names, affiliations & contact email.
- High-resolution figures (if any).
- Compliance with word limits and formatting.
