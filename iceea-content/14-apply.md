# How to Apply – ICEEA-ICETS 2026

Follow the step-by-step process to register and participate in the conference.

### Step 1: Visit Official Website
Visit the official portal and review themes, guidelines, and important dates.

### Step 2: Prepare Paper / Abstract
Prepare your research paper as per conference format.

### Step 3: Submit Your Work
Submit your paper via portal or email.

### Step 4: Review & Acceptance
Your paper will be reviewed and acceptance will be notified.

### Step 5: Registration & Payment
Complete registration by paying the required fee.

## Indian Delegates
- Submit abstract/paper
- Receive acceptance
- Pay registration fee (INR)
- Attend conference

## International Delegates
- Email submission + passport details
- Get invitation letter
- Complete international payment
- Join globally
