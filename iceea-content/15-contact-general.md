# General Queries — ICEEA-2026 & ICETS-2026

Information, FAQs and quick contacts related to the conference (26–30 October 2026) at Central University of Karnataka, Kalaburagi.

## About the Conferences

ICEEA-2026 aims to bring together researchers, academicians, environmentalists, policymakers, and industry leaders to address complex Earth and environmental challenges emerging during the Anthropocene epoch. The conference promotes interdisciplinary dialogue focusing on climate change, sustainability, natural resources, geosciences, environmental degradation, and advanced technological solutions.

### About ICETS-2026
ICETS is an internationally recognized congress focusing on environmental and territorial studies. The 18th edition continues its mission to build scientific collaborations between nations for sustainable environmental governance and resilient socio-ecological systems.

### Conference Goals
- Address global environmental challenges of the Anthropocene
- Strengthen international collaboration in Earth and environmental sciences
- Promote sustainable development and geo-environmental solutions
- Integrate geoscience, environmental policy, and technology
- Exchange cutting-edge research in climate change, geology, biodiversity, energy, and territorial studies

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: Who should attend?**
A: Researchers, faculty, graduate students, policymakers, industry professionals, and NGOs working on environment, geoscience, climate and territorial studies.

**Q: What are the submission & publication options?**
A: Accepted papers will be considered for a Springer edited book (ISBN) and for a Scopus-indexed journal (details announced later). All papers undergo peer review.

**Q: Are there workshops and field visits?**
A: Yes — workshops and a field visit are scheduled during the conference. Workshop modules, registration and fieldwork details will be published soon.

**Q: What is the review model?**
A: Single-anonymous peer review with at least two independent reviewers per full paper. Decisions: Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, Reject.

**Q: How do I register?**
A: Registration details and fees will be posted on the conference website. Early-bird deadlines and categories (student, academic, industry) will be announced.

**Q: Can I present remotely?**
A: Hybrid presentation options may be available for exceptional circumstances; contact the organisers with justification.

**Q: Accommodation & travel assistance?**
A: A list of recommended hotels and local travel guidance will be posted. Limited travel grants or waivers may be available for selected participants (details later).
